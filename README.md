# Free Books (CS)

Repository that collects and organizes computer science books and resources. The idea is simple: keep a convenient index by topic/title and link to PDFs or the original sources where they are available.

> Practical note: depending on your browser, the PDF may open in a new tab. Use “Save as…” if you want to keep a copy.

---

## Project purpose
- **Educational and reference**.
- **No monetization**: no paywalls, ads, affiliate links, or selling content.
- **No ownership claimed**: each work belongs to its authors and/or rights holders.
